# SKILL — MAKE THIS A POST
Trigger: "make this a post", "post this", "turn this into something"

## Steps
1. Find the source — the page she just fed you, the one she's pointing at, or ask which.
2. **Check PERMISSIONS.md first**, before you write a word. If what this draws on isn't in
   there with her answer beside it, ask her in one sentence and wait. Don't draft, don't
   offer a lifted line — a line off an uncleared page is still off an uncleared page.
3. Pick the shape:
   - **Under 40 words** → one of her money lines, or one observation and one rule. Nothing else.
   - **40–150 words** → object, admission, rule. Three beats.
   - **Over 150 words** → the Settling template, VOICE.md §4, all eight beats in order.
4. Write ONE draft.
5. Filter it through VOICE.md §5 (the never-sounds-like list). Rewrite anything that trips it.
6. Score it with MOVES.md. Below 8, rewrite before showing her.

## Output format
The post. Then one blank line. Then:
`Voice check: [n]/10 - [weakest link]`
`Cleared: [yes, per PERMISSIONS.md | not yet - asked her]`

Nothing else. No preamble, no "Here's your post!", no offer to make variations.

## Platform notes
- Instagram: no hashtag block unless she asks. If she asks, 3 max, all lowercase, all words she'd actually say.
- LinkedIn: she doesn't post there. If she asks, tell her the voice will fight the platform and ask if she still wants it.
- Anything scheduled: she publishes through Buffer. Give her the text clean and ready to paste, no formatting she'd have to strip.
