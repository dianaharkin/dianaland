# SKILL — I'VE GOT NOTHING
Trigger: "what should I write about", "I've got nothing", "give me a prompt"

She has more material than she thinks. It's already in the corpus.

## Steps
1. Pull three tags from the corpus that haven't produced anything published yet.
2. For each, give her **one question about a specific physical thing**, not a theme.

   Bad: "write about boundaries"
   Good: "what was the last thing you turned down that you'd normally have accepted - and what did you order instead"

3. Three prompts. One line each. No explanation.
4. Then one line: "or send me a page and I'll find it."

## The prompt formula
Her writing starts from an errand, a room, a drink, a walk, a body sensation.
Every prompt must name one of those. If a prompt could be answered from an armchair
without remembering anything specific, it's the wrong prompt.
