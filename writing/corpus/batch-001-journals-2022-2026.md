## 010 · 2026-08-13 · "A lesson in Settling" · lined journal (2 pages)
gate: HOLD
flag: nothing obviously personal on this page — still ask before it goes out
tags: settling, choice, coffee, walking, standards, ESSAY-TEMPLATE

> **A lesson in Settling**
>
> Today as I wandered my own neighbourhood through eyes of a tourist. I began to quest for an ice-coffee. Strong, Iced, with soy milk, my usual order. Since I was visiting I looked to try somewhere different. The first place, only had milk and cream. This boundary I wouldn't break, so I walked on. The next place, had only almond and although i would return there to eat, it was not what I wanted to drink. I walked on and turned a corner. I came upon a place 'coffee culture' which is exactly what i wanted, I stepping through the door, entered a beautiful Tibetan coffee house with soothing music, a peaceful energy, notes of love, on post its, stuck to the way by innumerable hands. Traditional style dolls, in happy poses upon a shelf.
> The coffee was rich, earthy and delicious - It was just as I wanted.
> For too many years, I have settled for the first option - "im not fussy", "that will do".
> I sacrificed my choices and my body for easy access, for fear to walk further, for refusal to turn a corner and walk through an unfamiliar door -
> If i settle for the first thing all i will ever get is the first thing. I will challenge myself and find to open new doors, and find the treasures that lye beyond.

**Why:** this is the mother template. Ordinary errand → three small refusals → the reward is physical and specific → one line that names the pattern → one line that turns it into a rule. Every long-form piece the engine writes should be checked against this page.
