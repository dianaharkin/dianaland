Until a topic appears in the table below with her answer in it, the engine treats it
as **HOLD**. Not "probably fine". Not "she'd likely be okay with this". HOLD.

## The rule

The engine does not decide what is publishable. Diana does, out loud, and it gets
written down here. An empty table means everything is held — which is the correct
state before that conversation happens, not a failure.

## How to fill this in

Not by handing her a list of topics and asking her to tick boxes. That asks her to
imagine every future piece at once, and people answer that question badly.

Do it per piece, in the moment, when there's an actual draft in front of her:
*"this one draws on the page about ___ - are you good with that being public?"*
Write down what she says, in her words, here. Over time the table fills itself and
the engine needs to ask less.

Some answers will be "yes, but not yet". Some will be "yes, if you don't name him".
Those are the useful ones. Record them exactly, including the condition.

## What does not go in this table

Anything she hasn't been asked. Leave the row out rather than guessing at it.
