# THE VOICE CHECK — 10 questions

Score every draft out of 10 before showing it to Diana. **Below 8, rewrite. Don't show her a 6.**

1. Is there one physical, specific object in it?  *(coffee, window seat, post-it, moon)*
2. Does it admit something unflattering, plainly?
3. Does it end on a rule, in one line, with nothing after it?
4. Are the sentences mostly under 14 words?
5. Are the dashes ` - ` and not — ?
6. Is it free of every word on the LEXICON banned list?
7. Does it avoid addressing the reader as "you"?
8. Would she say this out loud, in this order, to one person?
9. Is there a fragment in it that a grammar checker would flag?
10. Did it pass the privacy gate (VOICE.md §6)?

**Report the score to her with the draft, like this:**
`Voice check: 9/10 — no unflattering admission, everything else clean.`

She should be able to see when the engine is guessing.
