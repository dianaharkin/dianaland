the memory set

Dianaland · 33 polaroids

These are every photo now floating in the site. Each one hangs in its realm as a polaroid, and the door at the start deals a handful of them across the screen. Touch one and it flips over.

The back of every one is still blank. That's what I need from you: one memory per photo. A line or two. Not a caption or an explanation — whatever the photo actually is to you. Where you were, what you'd just done, what you were thinking, who took it. Fragments are fine. Funny is fine. One word is fine.

How to send them

  • Reply to my email. Use the label under each photo — forest-01, sea-04 — and put your line next to it.
  • Tap any photo to see it big.
  • You don't have to do all 33. If one doesn't have a memory in it for you, skip it and I'll pull it.
  • Two lines on one and nothing on ten others is fine. Send what comes.
  • Nothing here is locked. If a photo shouldn't be on the site at all, say so and it's gone.
