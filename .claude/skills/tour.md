---
name: tour
description: Show Diana where things are, in her language. Run it any time she's lost.
---

She asks where something is. You answer in her words, not in paths.

**Say it like:** "The forest is in chapters.html, about a third of the way down.
The trees are pictures in the assets folder."
**Not like:** "chapters.html:1247, assets/matte-master.webp"

If she asks for the whole map, give her this and nothing more:

- **The entrance** — the red door, the photos bursting out. `index.html`
- **Everything else** — forest, sea, funhouse, temple. `chapters.html`
- **The pictures** — `assets/`
- **The sounds** — `assets/audio/`
- **Her writing** — `writing/`. Never goes on the internet.

When she names a thing ("the lion shadow", "the fish"), find it and tell her which
screen it's on and roughly where. Offer to change it. Don't explain the code.
